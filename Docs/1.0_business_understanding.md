## 1.1 Business Background

Academic institutions continuously seek to improve student outcomes while efficiently allocating educational resources. However, many schools rely on intuition rather than evidence when identifying students who may need additional support.

By analyzing student performance data, schools can identify the factors most strongly associated with academic success and intervene earlier to improve educational outcomes.

## 1.2 Business Problem Statement

Despite collecting large amounts of student data, educational institutions often lack actionable insights into the factors influencing academic performance. Without data-driven decision-making, identifying at-risk students and designing effective interventions becomes challenging.

This project aims to uncover the key drivers of student success and provide evidence-based recommendations for improving academic performance.

## 1.3 Business Objective

The primary objective is to analyze student performance data to determine the factors that significantly influence academic achievement and translate those findings into actionable recommendations for educators and decision-makers.

## 1.4 SMART Objectives

### Our project objectives are:

- Specific: Identify the academic, behavioral, and demographic factors associated with student performance.
- Measurable: Quantify relationships using descriptive statistics, SQL analysis, and interactive dashboards.
- Achievable: Analyze the provided dataset of 5,000 student records using SQL, Excel, Power Query, and Power BI.
- Relevant: Support data-driven educational decision-making.
- Time-bound: Complete the full analytics lifecycle within this capstone project.

## 1.7 Assumptions

To ensure valid interpretation, we'll assume:

The dataset accurately reflects student records.
Scores are recorded consistently.
Attendance percentages are reliable.
Missing or inconsistent values (if any) are manageable.
The sample is representative of the population of interest.
1.8 Risks and Limitations
The dataset may be synthetic or anonymized.
Correlation does not imply causation.
Some potentially influential variables (e.g., socioeconomic status, teacher quality, mental health) are not included.
Findings may not generalize beyond the represented student population.

Recognizing these limitations demonstrates analytical maturity.
